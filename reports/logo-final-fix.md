# Final logo correction decision

The supplied logo reference was treated as the source of truth. The earlier SVG was not simply clipped: its geometry differed from the supplied mark. The corrected vector now reproduces the intended composition and uses a safe intrinsic canvas.

The founder portrait remains on the navy/mineral treatment. White was rejected because it would weaken visual integration and make the image feel more like a detached cut-out.

The six visible preliminary legal-review banners were removed as requested. The legal content itself remains otherwise unchanged.
