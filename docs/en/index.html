01

Critical Project Recovery & Troubleshooting

Restore control when a project is blocked, delayed or unstable.

The first task is not more reporting. It is to determine what is actually preventing progress, which decisions matter and how execution can restart.

Typical situations

  • Repeated delay without agreement on the cause
  • Failures involving several disciplines or suppliers
  • Escalation between client, contractor, engineering and site
  • Recovery plans that do not change daily execution

Support may include

  • Evidence review and root-cause diagnosis
  • Technical and operational risk mapping
  • Recovery options and decision points
  • Stakeholder alignment, execution control and verification
02

Commissioning, Start-up & Operational Readiness

Move from installed equipment to a functioning operation.

Commissioning exposes unresolved interfaces. Progress depends on coordinating equipment, controls, process conditions, people, procedures and acceptance requirements as one system.

Typical situations

  • Installation is advanced but readiness remains unclear
  • Mechanical, electrical, instrumentation and automation interfaces are misaligned
  • Punch-list closure is slow or ownership is fragmented
  • The plant cannot achieve stable operation

Support may include

  • Readiness reviews and commissioning planning
  • Installation supervision and precommissioning
  • Functional testing and multidisciplinary integration
  • PLC, controls, start-up and handover support
03

Industrial Service, After-Sales & Lifecycle Support

Protect the customer relationship after delivery.

After-sales performance is where technical reliability, commercial trust and future revenue meet. Cirta Capital can reinforce or temporarily lead the service function when capability or structure is under pressure.

Typical situations

  • Complex incidents exceed the existing team’s capacity
  • Service ownership and escalation paths are unclear
  • Customer commitments are disconnected from engineering priorities
  • Senior service leadership is needed without a full-time hire

Support may include

  • Fractional after-sales or service leadership
  • Escalation governance and case prioritisation
  • Customer communication and commitment tracking
  • Maintenance, spare-parts and lifecycle strategy
