2. Information collected

This website does not create user accounts and does not include server-side form processing. The contact form prepares an email in the visitor’s own email application. Cirta Capital receives only the information the visitor chooses to send, such as name, company, business email, country, project stage, urgency and a description of the situation.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Information is used to assess and respond to enquiries, communicate about potential or existing professional services, protect legitimate business interests and comply with applicable legal obligations. Where European data-protection law applies, processing may rely on steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests or legal obligations, depending on the context.

4. Confidential and technical information

Visitors should avoid sending highly sensitive or confidential technical information in the first email. Cirta Capital may propose an appropriate channel or confidentiality arrangement before detailed documents are exchanged.

5. Retention

Enquiry information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a business relationship, keep appropriate records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations.

6. Sharing, hosting and international transfers

Website hosting is provided through GitHub Pages. In delivering and securing the site, GitHub may process technical request data such as IP addresses, browser information and server logs under its own terms. This configuration does not provide Cirta Capital with visitor-level analytics. Information sent by email may also be processed by the sender’s and Cirta Capital’s email providers. Enquiry information may be shared with professional advisers or specialists involved in assessing an assignment when appropriate and subject to reasonable confidentiality expectations. International work may involve processing in the United States or other countries; appropriate safeguards should be assessed where legally required.

7. Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, and to complain to a competent authority. Requests can be sent to the privacy contact above.

8. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This first version does not use analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies. It does not intentionally store information in the browser for tracking purposes.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect business communications. No internet or email transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, service providers or legal requirements change.
