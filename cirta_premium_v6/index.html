About the owner

Engineering depth. Executive judgment. International execution.

Aghiles Abdi founded Cirta Capital to give industrial companies direct access to senior support that can move comfortably between the boardroom, the customer meeting and the plant floor.

His career spans industrial electronics, automation, commissioning, wastewater treatment, after-sales management, technical consulting and international customer success. He has held responsibilities at DEISA/COMSA, Condorchem Envitech and TraceLink, and later operated as an independent industrial consultant before establishing Cirta Capital LLC.

His work combines technical diagnosis, operational leadership, commercial awareness and multicultural communication. Engagements may involve customer escalations, multidisciplinary teams, contractors, commissioning programs, PLC systems, proposals, training and the recovery of difficult projects.
