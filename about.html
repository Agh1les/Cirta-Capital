Aghiles Abdi

Founder, Owner and Principal Industrial Consultant

Aghiles Abdi is an industrial electronics engineer and international operations professional whose career spans commissioning, automation, after-sales leadership, project delivery, technical consulting, customer success and business development.

His value lies in connecting disciplines that are often separated: process, controls, field execution, customer communication, commercial judgment and operational leadership. That combination allows him to diagnose the real constraint, align stakeholders and move a difficult situation toward a controlled outcome.

From 2023 to 2025, he worked independently, providing external leadership and specialist support for industrial after-sales operations, complex customer issues, proposals, commissioning and technical interventions. In 2026, he established Cirta Capital LLC to deliver this work through a dedicated international B2B company.
