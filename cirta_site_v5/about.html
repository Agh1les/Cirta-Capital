About Cirta Capital

The operator behind the advice.

Cirta Capital is a founder-led industrial consulting firm built around direct accountability, multidisciplinary judgment and international execution.

Clients work with the person whose experience, reputation and judgment stand behind the engagement.

Founder & owner

Aghiles Abdi

Industrial engineer, operations leader and international problem-solver.

Aghiles Abdi has worked at the intersection of industrial automation, commissioning, after-sales, customer success and project delivery since 2012. His assignments have required him to move comfortably between the control cabinet, the process, the project plan, the commercial offer and the customer boardroom.

His career includes operational and technical leadership roles with DEISA / COMSA Corporation and Condorchem Envitech, followed by international technical consulting for TraceLink and major pharmaceutical accounts. From 2023 through 2025, he worked as an independent industrial consultant, leading after-sales activities, complex customer escalations, commissioning work, technical proposals and specialist interventions.

In 2026, he founded Cirta Capital LLC to deliver that combination of engineering, business judgment and cross-cultural leadership directly to industrial clients worldwide.
