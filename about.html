About Cirta Capital

Industrial credibility is built in the field.

Cirta Capital was founded to give industrial companies direct access to senior technical and operational expertise without unnecessary layers between the problem and the person accountable for solving it.

Founder and principal consultant

Aghiles Abdi

Aghiles Abdi is an industrial engineer and international operations professional whose career spans industrial electronics, automation, commissioning, wastewater treatment, after-sales management, technical consulting, presales engineering and international customer success.

He began his career in industrial project and operations roles with DEISA and COMSA, progressing from deputy project management to supervision and management of international operations. His work included plant start-up, electromechanical troubleshooting, process monitoring, training, public-sector coordination and extensive on-site delivery.

At Condorchem Envitech, he worked as a technical engineer, start-up manager and after-sales manager. His responsibilities included international commissioning, PLC development and optimization, FAT and SAT activities, qualification support, technical offers, spare parts, maintenance contracts and key-customer management.

He later joined TraceLink as a technical consultant supporting international projects and major pharmaceutical accounts, combining technical advisory, customer success, training, problem resolution and commercial opportunity development.

From 2023 to 2025, he operated as an independent industrial consultant, including fractional leadership of after-sales activities, customer escalations, technical-commercial work, commissioning and specialist interventions. Cirta Capital LLC was formed in 2026 to continue and expand this work through a structured international B2B platform.

His professional approach is defined by direct responsibility, practical problem solving, multicultural communication and the ability to connect technical detail with operational and commercial priorities.
