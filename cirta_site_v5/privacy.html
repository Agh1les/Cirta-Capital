Who we are

Cirta Capital LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company providing international industrial consulting and related professional services. You can contact us at contact@cirtacapital.com.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, business contact details, company, project enquiry and correspondence. Basic technical data may also be processed by our hosting and security providers.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess potential engagements, prepare proposals, deliver contracted services, maintain business records, secure the website and comply with legal or contractual obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with providers supporting hosting, email, accounting, contracting, payments or professional advice, only where reasonably necessary.

Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably required for the purpose collected, contractual records and applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and record-retention requirements.
